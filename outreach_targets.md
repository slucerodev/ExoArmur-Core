## CONTACT BEST PRACTICES

### For Academics:
- Use university email addresses when available
- Reference specific recent papers or talks
- Offer collaboration, not just sales pitch
- Be clear about what you're seeking (validation, guidance, co-authorship)

### For Startups:
- Reference their recent funding or product launches
- Focus on partnership/complement, not competition
- Offer pilot program with clear value proposition
- Be specific about integration possibilities

### For Influencers:
- Reference specific content they've created
- Offer guest content or expert commentary
- Keep initial ask low (feedback, not promotion)
- Follow up on social media after email

### For Open Source Maintainers:
- Reference specific projects they maintain
- Focus on technical collaboration
- Offer contributions, not just use of their work
- Be respectful of their time (volunteer maintainers)
